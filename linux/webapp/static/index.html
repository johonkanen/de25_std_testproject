Loop details

-

PI on the hottest sensor, once a second; the fan speed stays within 1500..6000 rpm (the MAX6650 holds the speed it is given). The fan can only cool: at minimum fan and still below the setpoint, or at full fan and still above it, the loop is saturated and says so. If the controller stops, the fan keeps its speed and is raised to 4000 rpm after 10 s - the fan chip has no over-temperature fail-safe of its own.
